# ITU-R Recommendation BS.1770

- Source ID: `itu-r-bs-1770-loudness`
- Class: technical reference (international standards recommendation)
- Publisher: International Telecommunication Union, Radiocommunication Sector
- Title: "Algorithms to measure audio programme loudness and true-peak audio level"
- Editions: BS.1770-0 (2006) through BS.1770-5, approved November 2023, which is the edition in force
- Stable locator: https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770/en
- Retrieval: citation only — the ITU recommendation record page was retrieved and confirms the title,
  the issuing body, and the edition history; the normative text of the recommendation was not read

## Supports

- That a standardized algorithmic method for measuring audio programme loudness and true-peak level
  exists, is maintained by the ITU-R, and has been revised repeatedly since 2006.
- Its use in AudioMuse as the named reference for programme-loudness measurement in the Phase 12E
  measurement framework.

## Does not settle

- Any technical detail of the algorithm: the K-weighting filter, the gating rules, the measurement
  windows, or the definition of the LUFS unit. None of that text was retrieved, and no AudioMuse
  statement may describe the algorithm's internals on the strength of this source.
- Any relationship between measured loudness and perceived loudness in a musical context.
- Any relationship between loudness and emotion.
