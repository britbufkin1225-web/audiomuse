## Supports

- The phase vocoder was introduced by Flanagan and Golden by interpreting the vocoder filter bank as a
  sliding short-time Fourier transform.
- Time-scale modification and frequency shifting are early applications of the phase vocoder.
- The distinction Phase 12C needs: modern digital practice can separate duration from pitch, whereas
  changing playback rate on a turntable or tape machine cannot.
