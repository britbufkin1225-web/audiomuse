# The Screwtape Records

- Source ID: `popula-screw-tape-records`

- Author: Chris O'Connell
- Publisher: Popula
- Date: February 19, 2019
- Stable locator: https://popula.com/2019/02/19/the-screwtape-records/
- Retrieval: full text retrieved

## Supports

- Catalog practice at Screwed Up Records & Tapes: titles listed on whiteboards, ordered by name or by
  catalog number, with more than three hundred titles available and stock kept behind the counter.
- That by 2019 the shop sold CDs rather than cassettes, which is the format transition Phase 12C
  records for the circulation layer.
- That bootleg copies were a long-standing dispute and were sold in places Screw had no deal with.
- Reported trunk sales at very high daily volume, attributed to a participant rather than measured.

## Does not settle

- Original duplication mechanics. This source describes retail practice, not how masters were dubbed.
  Phase 12C therefore describes duplication in general cassette terms and marks Screw-specific
  duplication detail as not established by retrieved sources.
