## Supports

- KCOH began broadcasting May 5, 1948, and was purchased in 1953 by a group under Robert C. Meeker,
  after which it is described as the first Black-owned radio station in Texas.
- Decades of programming aimed at Houston's African American community, including interviews with
  national figures, and a street-facing studio window.
- Its 2012 sale, ending that era of operation.

## Does not settle

- Any connection between KCOH and later Houston hip hop radio. Phase 12C records KCOH as earlier Black
  radio infrastructure and explicitly does not assert a causal line to later rap radio.
