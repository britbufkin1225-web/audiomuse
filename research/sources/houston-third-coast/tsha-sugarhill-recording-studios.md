## Supports

- Bill Quinn established the facility in October 1941 as Quinn Recording; it became Gold Star Studios
  by 1950 and was renamed SugarHill Recording Studios in the 1970s.
- Lightnin' Hopkins recorded at least thirty-six songs for Quinn between 1947 and 1950; George Jones,
  Willie Nelson, and Freddy Fender also recorded there.
- Don Robey worked there from 1963 to 1973; International Artists leased and then bought the studio in
  1968, recording the 13th Floor Elevators and Red Krayola.
- Huey P. Meaux bought it in 1971; later owners include Modern Music Ventures (1986) and RAD Audio
  (1996); Destiny's Child recorded there.
- On October 8, 2011, it marked seventy years as the oldest continuously operating recording facility
  in Texas.
