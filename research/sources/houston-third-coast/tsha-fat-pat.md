## Supports

- Born December 4, 1970, in Houston; graduated from Sterling High School.
- One of the original members of DJ Screw's Screwed Up Click; performed with his older brother Big
  Hawk and with Lil' Keke during the mid-1990s.
- Fatally shot in Houston on February 3, 1998, after visiting a promoter's apartment to collect
  payment for a performance.
- The debut album *Ghetto Dreams* was released two weeks after his death on Wreckshop Records.
- Big Hawk, John Edward Hawkins, was shot and killed in 2006.
