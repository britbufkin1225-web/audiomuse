# DJ Screw Collection finding aid

- Source ID: `uh-dj-screw-collection-finding-aid`
- Class: institutional archive
- Institution: University of Houston Libraries, Special Collections
- Stable locator: https://findingaids.lib.uh.edu/repositories/2/resources/14
- Retrieval: full text retrieved during independent review

## Supports

- That DJ Screw's personal papers, photographs, creative material, equipment, sound recordings, and
  promotional material are held as a described archival collection.
- The claim that Screw-related material survives as documents and objects, not only as recordings.
- The finding aid describes custom requests, two slowed copies switched with a crossfader to repeat
  material, a master cassette, further slowing with a four-track pitch control, and home sales.
- It reports that Screwed Up Records & Tapes opened in 1998.

## Does not settle

- The finding aid does not give a month or Cullen Boulevard address for the 1998 opening, nor does it
  describe the equipment or workflow used to duplicate the final tape for sale.
