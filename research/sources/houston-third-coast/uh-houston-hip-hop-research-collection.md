# Houston Hip Hop Research Collection

- Source ID: `uh-houston-hip-hop-research-collection`
- Class: institutional archive
- Institution: University of Houston Libraries, Special Collections
- Stable locator: https://libraries.uh.edu/special-collections/houston-hip-hop
- Retrieval: full text retrieved during independent review

## Supports

- The existence of a university-held archival collection documenting Houston hip hop.
- Archives as an infrastructure layer in the Houston cartography.
- Corroborated by `uh-stories-dj-screw` and `uh-news-dj-screw-exhibit`, both retrieved in full, which
  describe the same collection, its curator, and its DJ Screw holdings.

## Does not settle

- Exact accession dates, extents, or item counts. Reported figures for the DJ Screw record holdings
  differ between accounts (roughly 1,500 versus more than 1,600 albums and singles), so this phase
  records the holding qualitatively rather than fixing a number.
