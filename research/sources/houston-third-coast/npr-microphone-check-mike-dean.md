## Supports

- Mike Dean's own account of entering Houston's music scene in the late 1980s and working extensively
  with Rap-A-Lot Records in the early 1990s, alongside N.O. Joe.
- That he functioned as both engineer and producer: mixing and sonic decisions plus played and
  arranged musical material.
- His stated preference for live instrumentation and elaboration over direct sampling, and the later
  adoption of MPC hardware.
- Work with the Geto Boys, Scarface, UGK, and Z-Ro.

- Dates for individual credits. Discography chronology in this phase is described in relative terms
  rather than pinned to years that this interview does not state.
