# Popular Rap Distributor Southwest to Close Doors

- Source ID: `allhiphop-southwest-wholesale-closing`

- Publisher: AllHipHop
- Date: February 5, 2003
- Stable locator: https://allhiphop.com/news/popular-rap-distributor-southwest-to-close-doors/

## Supports

- Southwest Wholesale operated as an independent rap-focused distributor for twenty-seven years and
  was among the largest independent distributors in that field.
- Layoffs began January 30, 2003, and owner Robert Guillerman was quoted on trying to stay open.
- Contemporary attribution of the collapse to internet distribution and bootlegging.
