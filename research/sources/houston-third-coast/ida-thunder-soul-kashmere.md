## Supports

- Conrad "Prof" Johnson directed the Kashmere Stage Band at Kashmere High School in Houston's Fifth
  Ward and brought funk and soul arrangements into the band from 1968, after a 1967 Otis Redding show.
- The band won national awards and performed internationally until Johnson retired in 1978.
- A track was sampled by DJ Shadow for a Handsome Boy Modeling School release; Stones Throw reissued a
  two-disc compilation in 2006; an NPR story about that reissue led to the documentary.
- Alumni reunited in 2008; *Thunder Soul* premiered at SXSW in 2010 and reached theaters in 2011.
