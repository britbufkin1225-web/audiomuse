# Keeping DJ Screw's Memory Alive

- Source ID: `uh-stories-dj-screw`

- Institution: University of Houston

## Supports

- Screw made and sold tapes from a house on Greenstone Street on Houston's south side.
- The University of Houston holds handwritten set lists, vinyl, photographs, original audio equipment,
  and Screw tapes, curated by hip hop archivist Julie Grob, and the Screw papers are the most
  requested collection in that archive.
- Screwed Up Click figures named include C-Note, Will-Lean, the Botany Boyz, Lil' Flip, Big Pokey,
  Big Hawk, Big Moe, Fat Pat, and Lil' Keke, who freestyled while Screw recorded.
- Screw recorded more than 300 original mixtapes and died in 2000.

## Does not settle

- Daily sales figures. The feature reports roughly 10,000 to 15,000 copies and about $15,000 per day
  at peak; other accounts give different numbers. Phase 12C treats volume as reported, not measured.
