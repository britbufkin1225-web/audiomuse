# C.I.A. Records (corporate entity record)

- Source ID: `uh-cia-records-finding-aid`
- Class: institutional archive
- Institution: University of Houston Libraries, Special Collections
- Stable locator: https://findingaids.lib.uh.edu/agents/corporate_entities/301
- Retrieval: citation verified, full text not retrieved (host refused automated retrieval)

## Supports

- That the Houston punk and DIY label C.I.A. Records, formed by the band Really Red, has an archival
  record held at the same university that holds the Houston hip hop collections.
- The narrow Phase 12C claim that Houston sustained an independent, self-released record economy
  outside rap as well as inside it.

## Does not settle

- Any influence relationship between Houston punk tape practice and DJ Screw. No retrieved source
  asserts one, so AudioMuse asserts none. Conceptual similarity is not evidence of lineage.
