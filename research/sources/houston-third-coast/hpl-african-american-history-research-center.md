# African American History Research Center (Gregory School)

- Source ID: `hpl-african-american-history-research-center`
- Class: institutional archive
- Institution: Houston Public Library
- Stable locator: https://houstonlibrary.org/aahrc
- Retrieval: citation verified, full text not retrieved

## Supports

- That Houston maintains a municipal archive dedicated to African American history, housed in the
  city's first public school for African American students, holding photographs, documents,
  recordings, and oral histories.

## Does not settle

- Building chronology and renaming dates, reported by secondary accounts as a 2009 opening of the
  library at the Gregory School and a 2022 renaming. Phase 12C names the institution as an archival
  layer without fixing those dates.
