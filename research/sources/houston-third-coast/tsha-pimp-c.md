## Supports

- Born December 29, 1973, in Port Arthur, Texas; his father was a trumpet player.
- Formed UGK with Bernard "Bun B" Freeman; released the cassette *The Southern Way* on the Houston
  label Bigtyme Recordz in 1988; signed with Jive Records in 1992.
- Produced all tracks on *Super Tight* (1994) and most of *Ridin' Dirty* (1996).
- Died December 4, 2007, in West Hollywood, California.

## Note on framing

- The entry calls UGK a "Houston rap duo" while also placing its origin in Port Arthur. Phase 12C
  keeps both facts and models the relationship as a Gulf Coast network link rather than resolving the
  tension by relocating Port Arthur into Houston.
