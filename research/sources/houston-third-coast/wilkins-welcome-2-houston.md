## Supports

- That Houston hip hop, slab car culture, and the relationship between place, identity, and music have
  a peer-reviewed scholarly literature by a Houston-raised folklorist who took a doctorate in folklore
  and ethnomusicology and co-founded the Houston Slab Parade and Family Festival.
- The Phase 12C decision to keep slab culture as a named, sourced boundary rather than an unsourced
  deep dive: this is the work a later phase should read first.

## Does not settle

- Slab etymology, vehicle acoustics, or any technical claim about car audio. Phase 12C deliberately
  makes none.
