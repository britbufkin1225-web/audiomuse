## Supports

- Shawn Brauch (designer) and Aaron Brauch (president) founded the firm after working for Rap-A-Lot
  Records, where outside design requests began arriving.
- Growth from an apartment to a 5,000-square-foot Houston office park facility by 1995.
- Clients including No Limit, Cash Money, and Suave House artists, and a volume of roughly twenty
  thousand covers before closing in 2003.
- Closure attributed to file sharing and to post-2001 travel disruption.

## Uncertainty and conflict

- **Founding year.** This source gives 1993; other accounts give 1992. Phase 12C records both.
- **Cover count.** Roughly 20,000 here; a figure of 19,180 appears elsewhere. Recorded as approximate.
