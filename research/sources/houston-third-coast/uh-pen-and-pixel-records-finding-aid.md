# Pen & Pixel Graphics, Inc. Records finding aid

- Source ID: `uh-pen-and-pixel-records-finding-aid`
- Class: institutional archive
- Institution: University of Houston Libraries, Special Collections
- Stable locator: https://findingaids.lib.uh.edu/repositories/2/resources/8
- Retrieval: full text retrieved during independent review

## Supports

- That the business records of a Houston album-packaging design firm are held and described by a
  university archive, which is why AudioMuse treats cover art as circulation infrastructure with a
  documentary record rather than as decorative trivia.
- Corroborated by a University of Houston digital collection of Pen & Pixel material.
- The finding aid dates the company to September 1992, names Shawn and Aaron Brauch as founders, and
  records their prior work for Rap-A-Lot.

## Does not settle

- The finding aid's September 1992 date conflicts with the 1993 account in `complex-pen-and-pixel`;
  Phase 12C records the disagreement instead of choosing.
