# DJ Screw: A Life in Slow Revolution

- Source ID: `walker-dj-screw-biography`
- Class: scholarly book (university press), built substantially from oral history
- Author: Lance Scott Walker
- Publisher: University of Texas Press, American Music Series, 2022
- Identifier: ISBN 9781477325131; 312 pages
- Stable locator: https://utpress.utexas.edu/9781477325131/
- Retrieval: publisher record retrieved in full; the book itself was not read for this phase

## Supports

- The technique description Phase 12C treats as authoritative in outline: spinning two copies of a
  record, chopping in new rhythms, bringing in local rappers to freestyle over the tracks, and slowing
  the recording down on tape.
- That the resulting cassettes sold in very large numbers in Houston.
- That the standard biography of Screw is an oral-history-based work, which is itself a reason to mark
  fine chronological detail as recollection rather than record.

## Does not settle

- Anything taken from the book's interior. Because only the publisher record was read, no page-level
