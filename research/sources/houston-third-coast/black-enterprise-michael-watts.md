## Supports

- Swishahouse was founded in 1997; co-founders named here are Michael Watts, OG Ron C, and G-Dash.
- Watts is credited with popularizing chopped-and-screwed music and hosted a late-night radio show on
  which he played his own mixtapes.
- Artists associated with the label's success: Paul Wall, Mike Jones, Slim Thug, Chamillionaire.
- Watts died January 30, 2026, at age 52.
