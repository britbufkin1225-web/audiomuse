# Vibration

Vibration is repeated motion around a resting state. When a vibrating source couples to air, a solid, or another medium, it can launch a disturbance that travels as sound.

Its rate, magnitude, shape, and damping help determine the resulting signal. Vibration therefore links physical sound sources to resonance, transducers, recording, and later signal analysis.
