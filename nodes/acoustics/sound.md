# Sound

Sound begins when a source vibrates and transfers energy into a medium. The resulting disturbance travels while particles mostly oscillate around their equilibrium positions; a receiver such as an ear, microphone, or sensor detects the change.

AudioMuse keeps the physical event distinct from perception. A microphone can detect sound without hearing it, while the auditory system interprets pressure variation as pitch, loudness, location, timbre, rhythm, and meaning.
