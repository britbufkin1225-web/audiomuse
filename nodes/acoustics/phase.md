# Phase

Phase locates a repeating motion within its cycle relative to a reference. When signals with related frequency content combine, their phase relationship can reinforce, weaken, or reshape the result.

Phase matters in microphone placement, stereo playback, synthesis, filtering, and digital processing. It connects physical timing to audible interference without being interchangeable with polarity or absolute delay.
