# Frequency

Frequency describes repetition rate: one hertz is one cycle per second. It is inversely related to period and, for a known propagation speed, determines wavelength.

Frequency strongly influences pitch but is not identical to it. Frequency is physical; pitch is perceptual, and hearing can infer tonal structure that is not represented by a single isolated frequency.
