# Synthesis

Synthesis creates and shapes sound through controllable processes rather than depending on the playback of an existing recording. Oscillators, filters, envelopes, modulation, and computational methods can organize frequency and time into evolving timbre.

It connects acoustic principles to electronic instruments and software. Sequencers and MIDI can control synthesis, while DSP supplies many of its digital building blocks.
