# Timbre

Timbre gives a sound its recognizable identity. Sounds with a similar pitch and loudness can remain distinct because their harmonic and inharmonic content, envelope, transients, noise, resonance, and modulation differ.

For electronic music, synthesis generates and reshapes timbre while sampling captures and transforms it. Timbre connects acoustic structure to perception and musical choice.
