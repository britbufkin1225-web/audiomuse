# Psychoacoustics

Psychoacoustics studies the relationship between measurable sound and what listeners hear. The auditory system organizes pressure changes into qualities such as pitch, timbre, rhythm, loudness, and location, influenced by context and expectation.

This distinction prevents AudioMuse from treating measurements as complete descriptions of experience. It also connects acoustics to music, recording decisions, DSP, and listening experiments.
