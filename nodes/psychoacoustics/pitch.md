# Pitch

Pitch is a perceptual quality rather than a frequency measurement. Periodic sounds often produce a pitch related to a fundamental frequency, but the auditory system can infer pitch from harmonic patterns even when that fundamental is absent.

Pitch connects acoustics to melody, tuning, synthesis, sampling, and musical-control data. Timbre and pitch interact because both depend on how the listener organizes spectral and temporal information.
