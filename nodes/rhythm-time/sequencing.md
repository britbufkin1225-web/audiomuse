# Sequencing

Sequencing represents musical behavior as ordered events: what happens, when it happens, and often how a parameter changes. Patterns can repeat, vary, and control instruments without containing audio themselves.

Analog sequencers, drum machines, MIDI, samplers, and software timelines make timing editable and repeatable. Sequencing is therefore a bridge between rhythm and electronic sound production.
