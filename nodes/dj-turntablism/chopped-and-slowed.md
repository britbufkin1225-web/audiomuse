# Chopped and Slowed

A digital editor can reproduce the audible outline of chopped and screwed and can also do things the original practice could not. That combination is why AudioMuse keeps this as a separate node rather than as a modern label for the same thing.

The mechanical differences are specific. Slowing in software may resample — reproducing the coupled behavior described on `slowed-playback` — or may time-stretch, changing duration while leaving pitch where it was, or shift pitch while leaving duration alone. The choice is a parameter, and the three results are audibly different. Repetition becomes slicing and looping: the repeat is placed rather than performed, lands exactly where it is put, and can be undone. The whole treatment is applied to a timeline that can be revised indefinitely, whereas the original practice produced a single recorded pass.

Relationships to the Houston practice differ case by case, and AudioMuse distinguishes four:

- **direct lineage** — practitioners working within the tradition, taught or influenced through it
- **indirect influence** — work shaped by the sound reaching a wider audience later
- **parallel technique** — slowing arrived at independently, since rate reduction is available on every playback device ever built
- **terminology borrowing** — the words travel further and faster than the practice does

Not every slowed track descends from DJ Screw, and claiming otherwise would be both unsourced and a poor description of how widely available the operation is. Equally, the terms themselves came from somewhere specific, and using them loosely erases that. The node stores no outbound relationships because nothing retrieved supports a directed claim from this later practice to another concept; its inbound edges from `chopped-and-screwed` and `time-stretching` carry what is supported.

Sourcing here is deliberately thin and is marked as such. This phase registered no source documenting the history of the term "chopped and slowed" or the internet-era slowed-music economy, so the node describes mechanism and category rather than chronology, and leaves the history explicitly open.
