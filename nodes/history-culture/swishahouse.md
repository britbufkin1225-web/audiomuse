# Swishahouse

Swishahouse was founded in 1997 and is the reason chopped and screwed practice did not end with the tapes that produced it. The obituary sourcing available for this phase names Michael "5000" Watts, OG Ron C, and G-Dash as co-founders, credits Watts with popularizing chopped-and-screwed music, and records that he hosted a late-night radio show on which he played his own mixtapes. Paul Wall, Mike Jones, Slim Thug, and Chamillionaire are the artists most consistently associated with the label's commercial success. Watts died on January 30, 2026, at fifty-two.

Two source problems are recorded rather than smoothed. Some accounts name only Watts and OG Ron C as founders, with G-Dash arriving later; and the obituary used here names a radio frequency that conflicts with the station commonly reported for Houston. AudioMuse therefore states the radio relationship without asserting a frequency, and states the founding lineup as unresolved.

The historically interesting claim is not that Swishahouse continued Screw's practice but that it changed the delivery system around it. Screw's tapes moved hand to hand and from a house and then a shop; Swishahouse mixtapes moved through a radio show, through retail CDs, and eventually through national releases. That is a different circulation mechanism carrying a related technique, which is why the stored edge is `influences` rather than a claim of descent. Describing the label as "DJ Screw continued" would erase both the north-side and south-side distinction the city itself maintains and the format transition that changed how the music was heard.
