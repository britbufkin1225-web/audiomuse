# AudioMuse Project Scope

## Canonical identity

- **Project:** AudioMuse
- **Repository:** `audiomuse`
- **Descriptor:** *A Resonant Atlas of Sound, Music & Signal*

## Purpose

Build an expandable atlas of sound across physics, perception, music, acoustics, recording, production, DJing, digital audio, DSP, synthesis, spatial audio, and machine audio.

## Scope principles

1. Keep the project repository-first and intentionally small.
2. Treat sessions as chronological exploration events.
3. Treat nodes as durable conceptual structure.
4. Connect theory to listening, visualization, experimentation, and practical use.
5. Preserve research provenance and distinguish source material from synthesis.
6. Add software only when it materially improves exploration or demonstration.

## Non-goals

- Large SaaS or content-management platform
- Generic music blog
- Placeholder-heavy encyclopedia generation
- Developer-first branding or cyberpunk presentation
- Unnecessary infrastructure
